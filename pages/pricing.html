Questions

Common questions

  • What happens when I hit my request limit?

    On Free and Starter, new requests are blocked at the included quota — upgrade for higher limits. On Growth and Scale, overage requests are charged ($0.50 / $0.25 per 1,000 requests respectively) so your service keeps running.

  • Do you take a percentage of my AI spend?

    No. A flat monthly fee only; TOLVYN takes no share of what you pay your providers.

  • Can I self-host?

    Not yet. On the roadmap for Enterprise customers.

  • Is there an SLA?

    No. No plan includes an SLA or an uptime commitment.

  • How can I try TOLVYN?

    The Free plan includes 10,000 requests a month, no credit card.

  • What counts as a request?

    One request equals one API call forwarded through the TOLVYN proxy to any AI provider — regardless of token count or model.
